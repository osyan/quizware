What we do now

Systematic Solutions focuses on interactive learning tools and school systems. Today, our main contribution is QuizWare, supported by our long history in quiz buzzer design.

QuizWare – free quiz control

QuizWare is our browser-based quiz control and scoreboard platform. It lets you configure teams, assign keys for adding or subtracting scores, and manage live quizzes with real-time updates.

It started as companion software for our USB quiz buzzer and now serves as a standalone tool for schools, trainers, and communities.

Works in a modern browser. No installation. Use it with laptops, desktops, or any setup that suits your venue.

From hardware to software

For many years, our USB Quiz Buzzer was our best-selling product in Hong Kong’s education sector. As technology moved forward and mobile devices became common, we recognised that better and more affordable ways to run quizzes were emerging.

Rather than rely on hardware alone, we transformed that experience into QuizWare, a browser-based quiz platform that works with devices people already own and offers a more flexible, long-term solution.

Our story

In 2006, the founder of Systematic Solutions designed the first prototype of the USB quiz buzzer, starting with self-use for training sessions, the CUHKAC Astronomy Quiz, and Bible quizzes at local churches. The reaction was immediate: participants were excited by the live projected display showing who buzzed first and each team's score, with feedback that was consistently an enthusiastic "wow!" The success of this trial made us realise there was a genuine need for such a device.

In 2009, Systematic Solutions was founded to provide the USB Quiz Buzzer as a formal product. It was one of the first of its kind on the market, bringing true plug‑and‑play USB quiz hardware into Hong Kong schools and organisations.

Our USB Quiz Buzzer quickly became the standard choice for quiz activities in local schools. Its compact design, simple USB setup, and ability to work seamlessly with projectors and computer screens made it much easier to deploy than traditional quiz equipment. Over time, with hundreds of units deployed, it established itself as a market‑leading quiz buzzer solution in the education sector.

Through ongoing discussions with teachers and organisers, we continuously refined both our software and hardware. We improved the quiz software interface for real classroom use, adopted mechanical keyboard switches for a more precise and satisfying tactile feel, and kept making the product better while maintaining our leadership even as similar solutions later appeared.

As technology evolved and mobile devices became common in classrooms and events, we recognised that quizzes no longer needed to rely on dedicated hardware alone. Rather than simply protect our existing USB Quiz Buzzer, we chose to out‑innovate ourselves by developing a new product that effectively replaced it. We transformed our experience into QuizWare — a browser‑based real‑time quiz platform that is more flexible, more convenient, and freely available to everyone, even though it ultimately surpassed our already successful hardware solution.

Today, the USB Quiz Buzzer has fulfilled its role, so we have decided to stop producing new units while continuing to support all existing customers who still use the system. Its place is now taken by a new generation of product — QuizWare — which we provide free of charge to the community as a way of contributing practical, proven quiz technology back to the wider education world.

KIDS – Kindergarten Intelligence Document System

Besides quiz tools, we also developed KIDS, a digital platform for early childhood education. It is a quiet, long-term project that we maintain for schools interested in structured digital workflows.

Built for kindergartens

KIDS is designed for kindergartens and early childhood centres that need a central place for student information, photos, and documents.

It aims to reduce manual paperwork and support day-to-day school operations in a structured way.

Core capabilities

Examples of what KIDS can support include:

  • Student data management and secure access
  • Photo upload and activity records
  • Teacher-written performance reports and approvals
  • Attendance recording and exception reporting

KIDS is ready for kindergartens and early childhood centres that wish to explore digital transformation in their daily operations, and we are happy to discuss it with interested schools.
